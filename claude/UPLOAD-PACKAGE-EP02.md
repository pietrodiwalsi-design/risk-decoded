## 2. Description (paste as is; no angle brackets, YouTube rejects them)

Someone may be copying your encrypted data today, not to read it now, but to open it once a big enough quantum computer exists. That strategy is called "harvest now, decrypt later". In about 9 minutes, Qubit and Glitch explain what actually breaks, whose data is exposed, a simple rule for whether you are already late, the deadlines governments have published, and what to do first.
