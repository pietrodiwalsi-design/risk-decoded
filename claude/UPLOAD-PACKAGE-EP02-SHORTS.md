## Short C — Mosca's rule
Title (pick one):
1. Are You Already Too Late for Quantum? Mosca's Rule in 50 Seconds  (recommended)
2. The 3-Number Rule for Quantum Risk
3. X plus Y bigger than Z: Are You Exposed?

Description (paste as is):

A simple rule of thumb for quantum risk: X is how many years your data must stay secret, Y is how long your migration takes, Z is how many years until a quantum computer can break today's public-key encryption. If X plus Y is bigger than Z, you are exposed. The numbers in the video (15, 5 and 10 years) are illustrative. Nobody knows Z, so plan with a range, not a date.

Full episode (9 minutes): The Quantum Threat, Harvest Now, Decrypt Later.
